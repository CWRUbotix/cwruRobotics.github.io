This year, our Micromouse team shifted their focus toward refining their PCB design and troubleshooting. Some changes to this year's board include a reworked JST pinout for motor connections, as well as inverted LIDAR encoders that now connect to the top of the board. Unfortunately, the team was unable to attend competition this year, but with a improved foundation in place, the Micromouse team is looking forward to hitting the ground running next year!
